How to Play

  • Complete a new word on each blank line. No duplicate letters may appear in any row.
  • Reuse all but 1 letter from the previous row and add 1 new letter.
  • No reused letter may stay in the same column when it moves to the next row.
  • Once a letter is discarded, it cannot be used again in that puzzle.
  • The keyboard contains a custom 8 or 9-letter set for the day's puzzle.
  • Some boxes contain fixed clue letters that cannot be changed.
  • Solve every row to complete the daily puzzle.
  • ROUND 2 unlocks an additional challenge after you solve the daily puzzle. You may play one ROUND 2 puzzle per day.
